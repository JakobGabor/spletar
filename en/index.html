FAQ

What people ask first

How long does it take?

Seven to fourteen days from the moment I have your details and photos. If you are in a hurry, say so and we will work to a tighter date.

You are in Europe. How does that work?

Everything runs by email and video call, the same way it would with someone two states over. I am on Central European Time, which means I reply within one business day and anything you send in your evening is usually answered by your morning.

What if I have no logo, photos or text?

That is the normal starting point, not a problem. I can design a simple logo, we write the text together, and for photos you get a shot list for your phone — I edit what you send back.

Do I pay every month?

No. The build is a one-time cost. The only recurring items are the domain and hosting, around seventy dollars a year, paid straight to the provider. Maintenance is optional.

Who owns the site?

You do. The domain is in your name and the site is yours. If you ever switch to someone else, you simply take it with you.

Can I edit it myself later?

Yes. At handover I show you how to change text, prices and photos. If you would rather not, send them to me and I will do it.

What about the domain and hosting?

I set both up for you — buying the domain, putting the site on it, getting the certificate working. You only tell me what the address should be.
